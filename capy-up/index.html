Capy Up: Stack to Space

A capybara soaks in a hot spring and does not mind what lands on its head. A bird carries oranges, rice balls, ducks and stranger things across the sky: tap to drop each one and build the tallest stack you can, through the clouds and out into space. One finger, short runs, plays offline, in English and Vietnamese.

Coming soon toGoogle Play Android · in testing

One tap

Drop each item on the stack. Land it dead centre for a combo; five in a row straightens the whole tower, ten earns a spare life.

Up to space

Climb past the clouds, the edge of the atmosphere, the Moon, the planets and the Milky Way, with a new bird at every stage.

Things in the sky

More than fifty items to stack, and odd silhouettes drifting past in the distance. Save your best runs and watch them again.
