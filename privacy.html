Privacy Policy: Bloat Float

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Bloat Float ("the game") is made by babybird ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is used when you play the game on Android and why. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (best distances, achievements, unlocked items, settings such as volume) is saved only on your device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show a rewarded ad when you choose to watch one, for example to continue a swim. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (for example, whether an ad loaded or was watched).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional Supporter Pack is bought through Google Play. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; we never see your card or payment information. The game only checks with Google Play whether the Supporter Pack is owned, so it can unlock it and restore it on a new device. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Crash reports

If the game crashes, Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance reports (device model, OS version, the error) if you have allowed this in your Android settings. These do not identify you.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone other than the Google services named above, which process it as described in their own policies.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
