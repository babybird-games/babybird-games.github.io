Privacy Policy: Hanafuda: Yokai Realm

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Hanafuda: Yokai Realm ("the game") is made by babybird ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is used when you play the game on Android and why. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's ad and Play services handle, described below.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (Dojo lessons, saved matches, unlocked card designs) and settings (language, volume, haptics, reduced motion) are saved in a file inside the game's private storage on your device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this file in your device backup to your Google account, so your progress can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads, for example between matches or when you choose to watch one for a reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (for example, whether an ad loaded or was watched).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases, such as removing ads or unlocking card designs, are made through Google Play. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; we never see your card or payment information. The game only checks with Google Play which items you own, so it can unlock them and restore them on a new device. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

The game uses internet access for ads and purchases, the advertising ID permission for ads, and vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's settings. Matches, lessons and the card collection work offline.

5. Crash reports

If the game crashes, Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance reports (device model, OS version, the error) if you have allowed this in your Android settings. These do not identify you.

6. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone other than the Google services named above, which process it as described in their own policies.

8. Deleting your data

The game has no account, so we hold no data about you to delete. Uninstalling the game removes everything it saved on your device. Advertising data is held by Google; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

10. Contact
