Privacy Policy: Hanafuda: Yokai Realm

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Hanafuda: Yokai Realm ("the game") is made by babybird ("we", "us"). The game does not collect, send or share any personal information. It has no account or sign-in, no ads, no purchases and no analytics, and it does not access the internet.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (Dojo lessons, saved matches) and settings (language, volume, haptics, reduced motion) are saved in a file inside the game's private storage on your device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this file in your device backup to your Google account, so your progress can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

2. Permissions

The game only asks Android for permission to vibrate, for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's settings.

3. Crash reports

If the game crashes, Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance reports (device model, OS version, the error) if you have allowed this in your Android settings. These do not identify you.

4. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal information from anyone.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

6. Contact

Questions about privacy: babybird.game.studio@gmail.com
