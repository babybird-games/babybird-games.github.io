Hanafuda: Yokai Realm

Hanafuda is Japan's 48-card flower deck: twelve months, one flower for each. Play Koi-Koi, the best-loved Hanafuda game, on a 3D tatami table with washi paper cards you can turn, flip and study up close. Plays offline with no account needed, in English and Vietnamese.
