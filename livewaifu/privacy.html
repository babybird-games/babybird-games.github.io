Privacy Policy: Live Waifu Wallpapers

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Live Waifu Wallpapers ("the app") is a live wallpaper app made by babybird ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses on Android. In short: the app collects nothing. It has no account or sign-in and does not connect to the internet.

1. Data stored on your device

Your choices (character, look, scene, frame rate and other settings) are saved in a file inside the app's private storage on your device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this file in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

2. Permissions

The app asks for no permissions and has no internet access. Android lets it draw a live wallpaper only after you choose it as your wallpaper.

3. Crash reports

If the app crashes, Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance reports (device model, OS version, the error) if you have allowed this in your Android settings. These do not identify you.

4. Advertising and purchases

The current version shows no ads and offers no purchases. If a future version adds them, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

5. Children

The app is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not collect your data, so we do not sell it or share it with anyone.

7. Deleting your data

The app has no account, so we hold no data about you to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on your device.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

9. Contact
